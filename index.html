Flagship project

Elbsociety

Event app for a Hamburg community · live in both stores

Shipped

The problem

Elbsociety is a community in Hamburg that organizes its own events. Everything ran through WhatsApp, which worked fine — until the group grew. Events got buried in the chat, nobody really knew who was coming, and organizing turned into guesswork.

What I built

A mobile app made for exactly this community. Members get a profile, browse upcoming events, confirm attendance with one tap, and create events of their own. Everything lives in one place instead of scrolling back through a chat.

My role

Self-directed and independently designed, from the first concept to the App Store release: product decisions, UI, front end, backend, deployment and both store submissions.

Stack

  • React Native (Expo)
  • Node.js / Express
  • Docker
  • Caddy reverse proxy

Einleben

Relocation companion for newcomers to Germany

Built, unreleased

Moving to Germany means visas, blocked accounts, registrations and insurance — a pile of bureaucracy with no obvious order. Einleben turns it into a guided journey: every step shows what it costs, how long it takes, which documents it needs and what to watch out for.
